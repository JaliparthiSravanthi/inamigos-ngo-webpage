About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is an NGO dedicated to creating positive social change through meaningful community-driven initiatives. The organization focuses on helping underprivileged communities, supporting children’s education, empowering women, protecting the environment, caring for animals, and creating better opportunities for youth.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Seva

Provides food, clothes, and basic support to people in need.

Project Bachpanshala

Supports education for underprivileged children and encourages learning.

Project Jeev

Focuses on animal welfare, care, and kindness towards animals.

Project Udaan

Empowers women through awareness, support, and opportunities.

Project Prakriti

Promotes environmental protection, cleanliness, and sustainability.

Project Vikas

Encourages skill development and career growth for youth.

Social Impact & Purpose

The purpose of these projects is to create a better and more equal society. Through education, donation drives, awareness campaigns, and volunteer participation, InAmigos Foundation helps communities grow with dignity, confidence, and hope.
