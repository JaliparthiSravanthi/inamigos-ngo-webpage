About the NGO

InAmigos Foundation is an NGO dedicated to bringing positive change in society through meaningful projects and community-driven initiatives. The organization focuses on helping underprivileged communities, supporting education, empowering women, protecting nature, caring for animals and creating better opportunities for people.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Seva

Provides food, clothes and basic support to people in need.

Project Bachpanshala

Supports education for underprivileged children and encourages learning.

Project Jeev

Focuses on animal welfare, care and awareness about kindness towards animals.

Project udana

Empowers women through awareness, support and opportunities.

Project Prakriti

Promotes environmental protection, sustainability and cleanliness.

Project Vikas

Encourages skill development and career growth for youth.

Social Impact & Purpose

The purpose of these projects is to create a better and more equal society. Through education, donation drives, awareness campaigns and volunteer participation, InAmigos Foundation helps communities grow with dignity, confidence and hope.
